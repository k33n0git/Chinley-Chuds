BREAKING: Chinley Chuds devastated by plane crash tragedy

Eleven players killed returning from playoffs as club mourns 'unimaginable loss'

17 September 2026 | 08:44 BST   |   Sky Sports News

The Chinley Chuds football club has been plunged into mourning after a plane carrying members of their squad crashed while returning from the recent Pro Clubs playoffs.

Eleven players are confirmed to have died in the tragedy.

Among those killed were four of the club's most prominent stars: Blyton (LAM), Jeggs (ST), Cinti (ST) and Quadrinaros (RAM).

Also confirmed among the dead are goalkeeper Junti, right-back Steven Flowe, centre-backs Trench and Cunby, left-back Weskoff, and defensive midfielders JoJo JoJo and Jo King.

The aircraft's pilot, Postman Pat, was the only survivor.

Chinley Chuds lose two of their greatest-ever players

Jeggs, the club's all-time leading goalscorer, had announced his retirement from football aged just 11 only hours before the aircraft took off.

Cinti leaves the club as its all-time leading assister, having established himself as one of the most creative players ever to wear the Chinley Chuds shirt.
